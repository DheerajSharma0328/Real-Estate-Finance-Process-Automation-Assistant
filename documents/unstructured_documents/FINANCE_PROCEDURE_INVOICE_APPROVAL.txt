Invoice approval procedure:
1. Confirm vendor and project.
2. Match invoice to purchase order.
3. Confirm cost code and budget availability.
4. Check required approval/supporting documents.
5. Record payment status.
6. Escalate mismatches rather than approving automatically.

A mismatch between invoice and PO requires review and supporting evidence.
